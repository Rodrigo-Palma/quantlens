# Quant & B3 Glossary

## RSI (Relative Strength Index)
A momentum oscillator bounded between 0 and 100 that measures the speed and
magnitude of recent price changes. RSI is computed with Wilder's smoothing over a
look-back period (14 sessions by default).

## Overbought RSI (above 70)
An RSI reading above 70 is conventionally called overbought: recent gains have
been large relative to recent losses. Overbought describes the size of the recent
advance; it is not a forecast that the price will fall.

## Oversold RSI (below 30)
An RSI reading below 30 is conventionally called oversold: recent losses have
been large relative to recent gains. Oversold describes the size of the recent
decline; it is not a forecast that the price will rise.

## Neutral RSI (between 30 and 70)
An RSI between 30 and 70 sits in the neutral range: neither overbought nor
oversold. Gains and losses over the look-back period are roughly balanced.

## Momentum
The total return of an asset over a trailing window (20 sessions by default).
Momentum is descriptive of past behavior and is not a forecast.

## Uptrend (positive momentum)
Positive momentum means the price is higher than it was 20 sessions ago: the
stock is in an uptrend over that window, with gains outweighing declines.

## Downtrend (negative momentum)
Negative momentum means the price is lower than it was 20 sessions ago: the stock
is in a downtrend over that window, with declines outweighing gains.

## Annualized Volatility
The standard deviation of daily returns scaled to a yearly figure (multiplied by
the square root of 252 trading days). It measures the size of price swings.

## High volatility (above 40% a year)
Annualized volatility above 40% means large daily price swings, wide ranges and
higher risk than a typical large-cap B3 stock.

## Moderate volatility (20% to 40% a year)
Annualized volatility between 20% and 40% is typical for liquid large-cap B3
stocks: ordinary daily swings and an average level of risk.

## Low volatility (below 20% a year)
Annualized volatility below 20% means small daily price swings, a calm and stable
price path and lower risk than a typical B3 stock.

## Adjusted close
The closing price adjusted for dividends, interest on equity (JCP) and splits, so
that returns across a distribution date are comparable. It is not the price that
traded on the exchange that day.

## B3 (Brasil, Bolsa, Balcão)
The Brazilian stock exchange, based in São Paulo. Tickers use a numeric suffix
(e.g. PETR4, VALE3) and the Yahoo Finance convention appends ".SA"
(e.g. PETR4.SA).

## Covered Call
An options income strategy where an investor who owns 100 shares sells a call
option against them, collecting a premium and capping upside above the strike.

## Cash-Secured Put
Selling a put option while reserving the full cash needed to buy the shares if
assigned. It generates premium income and can be a way to enter a position at a
discount, with risk comparable to owning the stock outright.
